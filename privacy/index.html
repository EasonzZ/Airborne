BondSet Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026. BondSet is published by Yichen Zhu.

Game data and iCloud saves

BondSet stores your game-generated player ID, profile name and selected in-game avatar, settings, tutorial progress, teams, tournament progress, and match and progression statistics on your device. When iCloud is available, the game uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to sync supported profile, progress, and settings data across your devices. Stamina and the game’s purchase-delivery records are stored on your device. If the iCloud account changes, sync is paused and the existing local save is kept instead of automatically merging it into the new account. Return to the original iCloud account and use the resume action in Settings to continue syncing.

Public league leaderboard

Public leaderboard uploads are off until you explicitly allow them in the league prompt or Settings. When enabled and iCloud is available, BondSet uploads eligible weekly league totals to Apple’s CloudKit public database. Records include a game-generated player ID, a system-generated public alias, your selected in-game avatar, the week and match statistics. BondSet does not upload your custom nickname to the public leaderboard. Other players can view the public contributions without signing into iCloud. Turning uploads off in Settings does not affect matches or local progression; local totals may still be shown privately alongside public rankings.

In Settings, “Delete My Public Records” stops future uploads and requests deletion of this player’s public leaderboard records. A second confirmation is required. Network, iCloud-account, or per-record failures are shown and can be retried; a pending request blocks uploads on this device. New app versions share a withdrawal marker through iCloud when available. Turn uploads off on your other devices and close older app versions before deletion, because older versions and already-sent requests cannot be recalled. This action does not delete local progress, stamina, purchases or private iCloud saves.

Local multiplayer and purchases

Local multiplayer uses Apple’s local network permission to discover nearby games and exchange room, team, and match data with the other device. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Apple processes App Store purchases. BondSet uses StoreKit to receive and verify transactions, deliver items, and restore eligible purchases. The game does not receive your payment card details.

Rewarded advertising

When rewarded advertising is available, Google AdMob provides ads and Google’s consent tools present privacy choices where applicable. Google’s advertising SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic and performance data for ad delivery, measurement, and related operations. Where tracking is requested, you can decline it or change your choice in iOS Settings. Where available, use Ad Privacy Settings in the game to update your advertising consent choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Optional usage analytics

If you choose to share usage data, BondSet uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand tutorials, matches, unlocks, stamina, and ad and purchase flows. The app sends game events such as team selections, scores, match duration and statistics, ad results and product identifiers. Google Analytics may also process an app-instance identifier, basic device, operating system and app information, approximate location and automatic usage or purchase events. The game does not attach your public leaderboard ID, nickname, or full receipts to its custom analytics events. We use analytics to improve the game. Analytics is separate from advertising consent, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time. Event data is available in Google Analytics and exported to a private BigQuery dataset in the US. The current BigQuery sandbox expires exported tables after 60 days. You can contact us about analytics data requests using the email below.

Retention and privacy requests

Local data stays on your device until it is removed. iCloud saves can persist separately from a local installation. Public league records can remain available after the week ends and after the app is removed unless deleted. You can initiate public leaderboard deletion in Settings as described above. To ask about access or other deletion requests, email zhuyichen716@gmail.com. For leaderboard requests, include your BondSet player ID from the game’s settings if available. Please do not send passwords or payment information. We use the information you provide to handle your request.

BondSet 隐私政策

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 16 日。BondSet 由 Yichen Zhu 发布。

游戏数据与 iCloud 存档

BondSet 会在设备上保存游戏生成的玩家 ID、资料昵称、所选游戏头像、设置、教学进度、球队、赛程、比赛统计和成长进度。iCloud 可用时，游戏通过 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储在设备间同步支持的资料、进度和设置。体力及游戏内的内购发货记录保存在设备上。iCloud 账号变更时会暂停同步并保留原本机存档，不自动合并到新账号；回到原 iCloud 账号后，可在游戏设置中恢复同步。

公开联赛周榜

在联赛提示或设置中明确允许之前，公开周榜上传默认关闭。开启且 iCloud 可用时，BondSet 会将符合条件的联赛周累计数据上传到 Apple 的 CloudKit 公共数据库。公开记录包括游戏生成的玩家 ID、系统生成的公开代号、所选游戏头像、周次及比赛统计，不上传你的自定义昵称。其他玩家可以在未登录 iCloud 时查看公开贡献。在设置中关闭上传不影响比赛和本机成长；本机累计数据仍可能与公开排名一起仅向你显示。

设置中的「删除我的公开周榜记录」会停止后续上传，并请求删除当前玩家的公开周榜记录，操作需再次确认。网络、iCloud 账号或逐条删除失败会明确提示，可再次重试；待处理请求会禁止本机上传。新版在 iCloud 可用时同步撤回标记。删除前请在其他设备关闭上传，并退出旧版游戏，因为旧版和已发送的请求无法被撤回。此操作不删除本机进度、体力、购买和私有 iCloud 存档。

局域网对战与内购

局域网对战使用 Apple 的本地网络权限发现附近的比赛，并与另一台设备交换房间、队伍和比赛数据。你可以在 iOS 设置中更改权限。App Store 内购由 Apple 处理。BondSet 通过 StoreKit 接收和验证交易、发放道具并恢复符合条件的购买。游戏不会收到你的支付卡信息。

激励广告

激励广告可用时，广告由 Google AdMob 提供，Google 的同意管理工具会在适用情况下提供隐私选项。Google 广告 SDK 可能处理根据 IP 推断的大致位置、设备标识符、广告与应用交互信息，以及诊断和性能数据，用于广告投放、衡量和相关运营。系统请求追踪权限时，你可以拒绝，也可以在 iOS 设置中修改。游戏显示「广告隐私设置」时，你也可以在那里更新广告同意选项。详情见 Google 如何使用采用其服务的应用提供的信息。

可选的使用情况分析

如果你选择分享使用数据，BondSet 会使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 了解教学、比赛、解锁、体力以及广告和内购流程。游戏会发送队伍选择、比分、比赛时长和统计、广告结果、商品标识符等事件。Google Analytics 还可能处理应用实例标识符、基本设备信息、操作系统与应用信息、大致位置，以及自动记录的使用或购买事件。游戏的自定义分析事件不附带公开榜单 ID、昵称或完整购买收据。我们使用分析数据改进游戏。使用情况分析与广告同意分别设置，你可以随时在游戏设置中关闭分析。事件可在 Google Analytics 中查看，并导出到美国区域的私有 BigQuery 数据集。当前 BigQuery 沙盒的导出表会在 60 天后到期。你可以通过下方邮箱联系我们处理分析数据请求。

保留期限与隐私请求

本地数据保存在设备上，直至被移除。iCloud 存档可独立于本机安装保留。公开周榜记录若未删除，可能在该周结束或卸载游戏后继续保留。公开榜单记录可按上文从游戏设置发起删除；如需查询或提出其他删除请求，请发邮件至 zhuyichen716@gmail.com。榜单相关请求可附上游戏设置里的 BondSet 玩家 ID。请勿发送密码或支付信息。你提供的信息将用于处理该请求。
